# Abstract

Vision-language navigation (VLN) for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) is a key task in embodied intelligence and has witnessed rapid progress in both indoor and outdoor research domains. However, existing studies have long treated indoor and outdoor scenarios in isolation, lacking adaptation to the intrinsic differences between the two environments. Such divergence stems from their inherent characteristics: indoor navigation emphasizes fine-grained manipulation and obstacle avoidance within constrained spaces, whereas outdoor navigation requires long-range planning and coping with dynamic complexities in large-scale environments. As a result, models restricted to a single domain suffer from limited generalization and practical applicability. To address these challenges, we propose AirUni, a unified UAV framework that supports both precise indoor operations and large-scale outdoor navigation. AirUni enables cross-domain collaborative learning through the construction of a large-scale joint indoor–outdoor dataset and the design of a scenario-adaptive training strategy. To further improve efficiency, we introduce a dynamic frame sampling mechanism with time-decay weighting to reduce computational cost, and a vision–text token interaction module to enhance temporal modeling. Extensive experiments demonstrate that AirUni achieves superior generalization and navigation efficiency across diverse indoor and outdoor tasks, highlighting its potential to advance UAV VLN toward real-world applications.
